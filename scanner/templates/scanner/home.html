Dependency scanner

Know what's vulnerable before attackers do

Drop in requirements.txt, pyproject.toml, package.json or a lockfile. Sentinel checks every package against public advisories in seconds. Nothing you upload is stored.

Scan dependencies
Six sources, one answer

OSV.dev, CISA KEV, RetireJS, NVD, CVE.org and the news

All six are asked at once. Duplicates are merged, and every finding shows which sources back it and how each one rated it.

Signal, not noise

Confirmed, possible, or not your problem

Exact versions are confirmed. Ranges like >=5.2 only show advisories that can reach them, and old bugs a plain update already fixes are counted separately. In our own tests that cut 719 alerts to 83.

Fix first

Exploited bugs and malware rise to the top

Anything on CISA's Known Exploited list or flagged as malicious code is never buried. A single table tells you which upgrade clears the most findings.

Reports and articles

Official advisories, kept apart from the headlines

CISA advisories and vendor bulletins sit in their own lane. News that names a CVE you actually have is marked, so a story about your stack never gets lost in the feed.

Read the latest
Private by design

Your files stay yours

Uploads are read in memory and thrown away. Results live only in your browser session. Only public lookups, like advisory records and feed copies, are saved to keep things fast.
